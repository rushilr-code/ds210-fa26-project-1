Paste your bashcrawl session below the line.

Everything you typed and everything the dungeon printed back, from the moment you
started to the moment you finished. Do not tidy it up. Wrong turns, typos and dead
ends are all fine, and demonstrate your real effort and process.

To get the text out of your terminal:

  On Mac, press command-S and save the window's contents to a file.

  On Git Bash, right-click inside the window and choose Select All, then
  right-click again and choose Copy.

Then paste it below the line. Do not close the terminal before you have saved.
There is no way to get the session back afterwards.
